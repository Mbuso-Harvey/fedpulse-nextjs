# Intelligence Product Strategy

Created: 2026-06-16 23:58:38

## Core Principle

The Federal Procurement Intelligence Network is not only a database.

It is a monetizable intelligence engine.

The goal is to turn procurement data into:

- Reports
- Alerts
- Benchmarks
- Rankings
- Market maps
- Renewal signals
- Competitor intelligence
- Forecasts
- Media-ready insights
- Research datasets
- Executive dashboards
- API products
