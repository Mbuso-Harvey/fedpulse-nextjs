# Renewal Watch Product Specification V1

Created: 2026-06-19 13:09:58

## Product Purpose

Help government contractors identify upcoming federal contract renewal opportunities before competitors.

## Target Customers

- Government contractors
- Capture managers
- Business development teams
- Proposal teams
- Consulting firms

## Core Customer Pain

- Missing renewal opportunities
- Discovering opportunities too late
- Not knowing incumbent suppliers
- Not knowing renewal timing
- Poor bid pipeline visibility

## Primary Deliverables

1. Monthly Renewal Watch PDF
2. Weekly Renewal Alert Email
3. CSV Opportunity Feed
4. Dashboard (future)

## Minimum Viable Subscription

- Monthly PDF
- Weekly alert email
- Top renewal opportunities
- Department rankings
- Incumbent supplier analysis

## Renewal Opportunity Record

Each renewal opportunity should contain:

- Department
- Supplier
- Contract value
- Contract category
- Original contract date
- Estimated renewal date
- Renewal confidence
- Opportunity score
- Incumbent concentration
- Notes

## Weekly Alert Rules

Only include:

- New opportunities
- Material value changes
- New high-value departments
- New high-value suppliers
- New expiring contracts

Do NOT repeat unchanged records.

## Freshness Rules

A customer should always receive meaningful new intelligence.

Each edition must include:

- New opportunities
- New rankings
- New department movements
- New supplier movements
- New renewal alerts

## Report Sections

1. Executive Summary
2. Top Renewal Opportunities
3. Department Renewal Rankings
4. Supplier Exposure Analysis
5. Upcoming Expiries
6. Market Changes Since Last Edition
7. Watch List

## Pricing Hypothesis

- Solo Contractor: $49/month
- Small Team: $199/month
- Enterprise Team: $999/month

## Success Metric

A subscriber should discover at least one actionable opportunity they would likely have missed.
