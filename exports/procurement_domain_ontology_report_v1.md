## Domain Definitions

### PDO-ACQ-001 - Acquisition Strategy

Procurement method, notice type, market research, solicitation strategy, competition posture, set-aside, and acquisition metadata.

Commercial Value: Opportunity qualification, bid/no-bid analysis, pipeline segmentation.

### PDO-SUB-001 - Submission and Proposal Management

Proposal volumes, submission procedures, amendment acknowledgements, clarifications, communications, and offer instructions.

Commercial Value: Proposal compliance, response planning, capture workflow automation.

### PDO-EVAL-001 - Evaluation and Source Selection

Award basis, evaluation factors, evaluation method, LPTA, best value, tradeoff, and source selection constraints.

Commercial Value: Win strategy, capture prioritization, evaluation framework intelligence.

### PDO-COM-001 - Commercial and Pricing

Pricing schedules, quote terms, contract type, payment, invoicing, unit prices, and commercial terms.

Commercial Value: Pricing benchmarks, pricing strategy, contract economics.

### PDO-SUP-001 - Supplier Eligibility and Qualifications

Supplier registration, authorization, certifications, past performance, references, and capability evidence.

Commercial Value: Supplier fit scoring, qualification gap analysis, vendor positioning.

### PDO-PERF-001 - Performance and Operations

Mandatory performance obligations, contractor responsibility, staffing, service delivery, and operational execution.

Commercial Value: Requirement understanding, delivery risk, supplier capability matching.

### PDO-LOG-001 - Delivery and Logistics

Delivery location, destination, shipment, transportation, site access, FOB, and logistics constraints.

Commercial Value: Delivery planning, fulfillment feasibility, cost estimation.

### PDO-COMP-001 - Legal, Compliance, and Labor

FAR/DFARS clauses, labor laws, Davis-Bacon, OSHA, privacy, PHI, subcontracting, and regulatory compliance.

Commercial Value: Compliance risk scoring, eligibility analysis, legal requirement extraction.

### PDO-TECH-001 - Technical Requirements

Products, materials, dimensions, equipment, technical clarifications, specifications, and product capability.

Commercial Value: Capability matching, product fit, technical gap analysis.

### PDO-DOMAIN-001 - Domain-Specific Operations

Industry or mission-specific requirements such as cemetery operations, healthcare services, base life support, and specialized agency missions.

Commercial Value: Vertical intelligence, niche supplier matching, domain-specific opportunity search.

### PDO-DOC-001 - Document Structure and Administrative Forms

Forms, OCR fragments, tables, cover sheets, SF-1449, table of contents, and administrative document metadata.

Commercial Value: Document cleanup, evidence recovery, false-positive reduction.

### PDO-UNK-001 - Residual Unknown

Expressions not yet assigned to a stable procurement domain.

Commercial Value: Discovery backlog and taxonomy expansion monitoring.
