# Procurement Platform Architecture V3

Created: 2026-06-26 03:04:13

## Strategic Vision

The Federal Procurement Intelligence Network is organized into three integrated platforms.

PLP → Procurement Language Platform

PKG → Procurement Knowledge Graph

PIP → Procurement Intelligence Platform

Each platform has distinct responsibilities while operating as one integrated system.

---

## Platform Mission

Transform procurement documents into explainable, evidence-backed commercial intelligence.

---

## Architectural Principles

1. Evidence before intelligence.

2. Canonical evidence before semantic learning.

3. Provenance is mandatory.

4. Intelligence must be explainable.

5. Unknown language enters discovery, not deletion.

6. Semantic learning expands the registry rather than replacing it.

7. Knowledge graph relationships must remain evidence-backed.

8. Commercial products consume knowledge graph objects rather than raw documents.
