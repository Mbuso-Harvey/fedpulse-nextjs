# Federal Procurement Source Catalog

Last Updated: 2026-06-16 17:58:47

## Purpose

This document records the official data sources for Phase 1 of the Federal Procurement Intelligence Network.

Phase 1 focuses only on Canadian federal procurement data.

## Discovered Sources

### 1. CanadaBuys Tender Notices

Purpose:

Collect federal tender opportunities.

Business value:

- Opportunity search
- Department buying behavior
- Category demand analysis
- Future opportunity scoring

Status:

Discovered, not ingested.

### 2. CanadaBuys Award Notices

Purpose:

Collect award notices and winning supplier information.

Business value:

- Supplier intelligence
- Competitor intelligence
- Award trend analysis
- Win concentration analysis

Status:

Discovered, not ingested.

### 3. CanadaBuys Contract History

Purpose:

Collect long-term historical contract data.

Business value:

- Renewal intelligence
- Spending intelligence
- Supplier intelligence
- Predictive procurement foundation

Status:

Discovered, not ingested.

## Strategic Decision

We will begin with official downloadable federal datasets instead of scraping.

This reduces maintenance risk and allows the platform to focus on intelligence creation.
