## Strategic Decision

Supplier identity accuracy is a core platform asset.

Supplier display formatting is not.

## Official Priority Order

1. Supplier Resolution
2. Alias Management
3. Supplier Family Detection

## Important Rule

Never sacrifice identity accuracy for cosmetic improvements.

## LLM Usage Policy

LLMs may be used for:

- Display names
- Encoding repair suggestions
- Alias suggestions
- Supplier family suggestions

LLMs may NOT be used as the source of truth for:

- Supplier IDs
- Entity resolution
- Contract matching
- Financial calculations
- Quality scoring
