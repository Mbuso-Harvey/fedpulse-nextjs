## Purpose

The AI Procurement Analyst synthesizes outputs from all certified
Wave 10 intelligence products into a single executive assessment.

## Inputs

- Opportunity Intelligence
- Supplier Intelligence
- Department Intelligence
- Similar Opportunity Engine
- Recommendation Engine
- Graph Analytics

## Governance Rules

1. Only certified upstream products may be used.
2. The analyst must remain fully explainable.
3. All recommendations must trace back to evidence.
4. Scores are advisory and do not replace human judgement.
5. Every assessment must be reproducible.
