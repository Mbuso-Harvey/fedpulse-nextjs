## Feature Freeze Doctrine

1. Wave 14 is the Version 1.0 feature freeze.
2. The certified intelligence engine is frozen after Wave 14.
3. The certified autonomous agent layer is frozen after Wave 14.
4. Production API contracts are frozen after Wave 14.
5. The certified demo baseline is frozen after Wave 14.
6. UI/UX implementation is launch execution and may proceed after the freeze.
7. First-client onboarding becomes the primary product priority after Wave 14.
8. Customer-critical defects, security fixes, and onboarding blockers may be corrected.
9. Speculative feature development is prohibited before first-client feedback.
10. New product development must be justified by real customer usage, onboarding requirements, retention needs, or demonstrated revenue opportunity.

## Post-Launch Discipline

The post-launch backlog is a holding area, not an active development plan.

Items may only move from the backlog into active development when supported by first-client evidence or a clear launch-critical requirement.
