# Wave 10 Intelligence Products Governance V1

Created: 2026-06-29 17:04:15

## Purpose

This governance layer controls the transition from knowledge graph infrastructure to commercial intelligence products.

## Rules

1. Intelligence products must consume KG-1.0 exports or derivative indexes.
2. Product outputs must preserve graph lineage where possible.
3. Search, recommendations, and analyst outputs must be explainable.
4. API outputs must cite product version and graph version.
5. No product should bypass semantic governance by reading raw procurement text directly unless explicitly marked experimental.
6. Every product must produce QA outputs before being promoted.

KG-1.0

## Active Product Version
