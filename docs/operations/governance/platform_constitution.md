# Requirements Intelligence Amendment

Created: 2026-06-21 21:11:28

## PRINCIPLE 0017 — Procurement Records Are Evidence, Not The Product

Procurement records, contracts, tenders, awards, renewals, attachments, evaluation documents, specifications, forms, schedules, amendments, requirements and metadata are not the final product.

They are evidence.

The purpose of the platform is to discover and model the underlying buying behavior, requirements, incentives, evaluation criteria, decision patterns and commercial opportunities hidden within those records.

The objective is not merely to report what happened.

The objective is to explain:

- What buyers want
- How buyers evaluate
- What suppliers require to compete
- What characteristics increase win probability
- What risks reduce win probability
- What commercial actions should be taken

The platform must always seek the underlying drivers behind the data.

## PRINCIPLE 0018 — Every Data Asset Is Presumed Valuable Until Proven Otherwise

No field, attachment, document, clause, schedule, form, requirement, certification, evaluation criterion, amendment, metadata element or derived attribute shall be discarded solely because it is not immediately useful.

Every data asset must first be evaluated for:

- Intelligence value
- Commercial value
- Predictive value
- Customer value
- Revenue potential
- Future model utility

The burden of proof is on exclusion, not inclusion.

The platform shall prefer complete discovery before simplification.

## PRINCIPLE 0019 — Revenue Engine Thinking

Every feature, dataset, model, report, dashboard, alert, score, recommendation and workflow shall be evaluated through:

1. Customer Pain
2. Data Asset
3. Deliverable
4. Pricing Logic
5. Repeatability

The platform must think like a revenue engine.

The question is not:

"What can this data tell us?"

The question is:

"What customer problem can this data solve repeatedly and profitably?"

## PRINCIPLE 0020 — Requirements Intelligence Is A Core Asset

Government buying requirements are a primary commercial asset.

The platform shall extract, preserve, classify and monetize requirements such as:

- Security requirements
- Clearance requirements
- Certifications
- Past performance requirements
- Staffing requirements
- Technical requirements
- Proposal instructions
- Evaluation criteria
- Pricing structures
- Forms
- Deliverables
- Compliance obligations

The platform must not stop at detecting that a requirement category exists.

It must extract the specific named requirement wherever possible.

Examples:

- Not merely "certification found"
- But "NIST SP 800-171"
- Not merely "clearance found"
- But "Secret Clearance"
- Not merely "evaluation found"
- But "Lowest Price Technically Acceptable"

## PRINCIPLE 0021 — Absence Of Evidence Is Not Evidence Of Absence

The platform shall not assume that proposals, evaluation documents, debriefs, source selection documents, performance records or award rationales do not exist merely because they have not yet appeared in a sample.

Unknown asset classes must be investigated before exclusion.
