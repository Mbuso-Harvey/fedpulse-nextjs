
# Federal Procurement Intelligence Network
# Commercial Intelligence Constitution

Last Updated: 2026-06-18 19:32:49

======================================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

The Federal Procurement Intelligence Network is not a data warehouse.

The Federal Procurement Intelligence Network is a commercial intelligence engine.

Data is the input.

Revenue-generating intelligence products are the output.

======================================================================
COMMERCIAL INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK
======================================================================

Every future sprint must connect:

Customer Pain
    ->
Data Asset
    ->
Intelligence Asset
    ->
Deliverable
    ->
Pricing Logic
    ->
Repeatability
    ->
Revenue

======================================================================
MANDATORY COMMERCIAL QUESTIONS
======================================================================

Before building any new module:

1. Who pays for this?

2. What customer pain does it solve?

3. What decision does it improve?

4. What deliverable is produced?

5. How is it delivered?

6. How often is it delivered?

7. What differentiates it from free information?

8. Can it be productized?

9. Can it be automated?

10. Does it support recurring revenue?

======================================================================
PLATFORM OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Do not maximize datasets.

Maximize valuable, repeatable, scalable,
revenue-generating intelligence products.

======================================================================
FIVE CORE QUESTIONS
======================================================================

Every major intelligence asset should help answer:

Who buys?

Who wins?

What expires?

Who should I target?

What is likely to happen next?

======================================================================
SPRINT PRIORITIZATION RULE
======================================================================

If two possible sprints exist:

Option A:
Higher commercial value

Option B:
Higher cosmetic value

The platform prioritizes Option A.

======================================================================
DOCUMENTATION RULE
======================================================================

Every future enhancement discovered must be recorded in:

- Product Backlog
- Technical Debt Register
- Roadmap Registry

before being deferred.
