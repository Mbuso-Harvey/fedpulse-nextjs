# Intelligence Constitution V1

Created: 2026-06-26 03:04:13

Rules:

- Intelligence is evidence-backed.

- Intelligence is reproducible.

- Intelligence is explainable.

- Predictions are confidence scored.

- Recommendations preserve provenance.

- Semantic models augment rules rather than replacing them.

- Human review remains available for discovery items.

- Platform learning improves continuously without losing auditability.
