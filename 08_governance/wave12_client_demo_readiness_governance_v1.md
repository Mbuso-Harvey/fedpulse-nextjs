2. The demo must show connected decision intelligence rather than isolated features.
3. Search and question answering must lead into opportunity intelligence.
4. The autonomous agent chain must be explained as decision support.
5. Human approval and commercial authority restrictions must be explicit.
6. The executive decision must demonstrate conservative risk controls.
7. Demo claims must be supported by certified platform artifacts.
8. UI/UX implementation must preserve the certified demo journey.
9. Wave 14 remains the Version 1.0 feature freeze.
10. After Wave 14, first-client onboarding and real customer feedback take priority over speculative feature development.
