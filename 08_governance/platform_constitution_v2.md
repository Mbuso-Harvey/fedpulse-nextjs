# Platform Constitution V2

Created: 2026-06-26 03:04:13

## Constitutional Principles

1. Evidence First

All intelligence originates from evidence.

2. Provenance Always

Every intelligence object must preserve traceability.

3. Canonical Before Semantic

Semantic learning consumes canonical evidence.

4. Explainable Intelligence

Every recommendation must be explainable.

5. Knowledge Graph Integrity

Relationships require supporting evidence.

6. Registry Growth

New procurement language enters discovery before becoming canonical.

7. Commercial Alignment

Every engineering sprint should support future commercial capabilities.
