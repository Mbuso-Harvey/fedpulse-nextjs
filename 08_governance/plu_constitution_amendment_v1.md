# PLU Constitution Amendment V1

Created: 2026-06-24 19:00:15

## Amendment: Procurement Language Understanding Is Core Infrastructure

The platform shall treat Procurement Language Understanding as a core infrastructure layer.

No specialized intelligence engine should permanently depend only on raw keyword extraction when a canonical PLU classification exists.

## New Principles

### 0022 Context Before Intelligence

The platform must classify the procurement context of a statement before deriving business intelligence from it.

### 0023 Raw Words Are Not Meaning

The same word can mean different things in different procurement contexts.

Example:
- Price Proposal is proposal structure.
- Unit Price is pricing structure.
- Price as an evaluation factor is award decision intelligence.

### 0024 Navigation Is Not Methodology

Section M, Evaluation Factors, and proposal volume labels are navigation signals unless they contain award decision logic.

### 0025 Exclusions Are Intelligence

Administrative noise, document markings, and non-procurement language must be explicitly classified and preserved for audit rather than silently discarded.

### 0026 Semantic Reuse Over Script Sprawl

Reusable PLU classifications should feed downstream engines to reduce duplicate parsing logic and inconsistent interpretations.
