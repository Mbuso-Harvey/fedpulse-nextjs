# Wave 10 Recommendation Engine Governance V1

Created: 2026-07-01 14:16:58

## Product Status

recommendation_engine_certified

## Purpose

The Recommendation Engine synthesizes opportunity, supplier, department, and similarity intelligence into prioritized procurement actions.

## Governance Rules

1. Recommendations must be generated from certified upstream intelligence products.
2. Every recommendation must include evidence source and explainability.
3. Every recommendation must have confidence, business impact, and priority score.
4. Bid/no-bid recommendations must use supplier fit and opportunity readiness.
5. V1 recommendations are advisory and must not replace human review.
6. Future versions will incorporate real tender history, supplier performance, and outcome feedback.
